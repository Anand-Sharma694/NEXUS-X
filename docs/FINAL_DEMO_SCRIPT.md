# NEXUS-X Final Presentation Demo

## 1. Problem
Paste a messy operational problem into Analyze.

## 2. Understand
Show generated tasks, effort, risks, dependencies and critical path.

## 3. Decide
Show health and explain WHY the project is healthy/at risk.

## 4. Simulate
Change deadline/team/scope in What-If and show the recalculated verdict.

## 5. Execute
Change a task to BLOCKED. Refresh the dashboard and show risk/health/notification changes.

## 6. Ask
Ask Copilot: `Why is the project at risk and what should I do first?`
Then ask a general question to demonstrate the general assistant mode.

## 7. People
Add a team member and show availability/workload.

## 8. Evidence
Open Risks, Monitor, Signal Lab and Activity to show WHY/EVIDENCE/ACTION.

## 9. Export
Generate the PDF report and CSV task export.

## 10. Build
Generate a project ZIP and explain that it includes code, tests, specification and documentation.
